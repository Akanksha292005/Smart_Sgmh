Privacy notice

Prototype notice. The hospital's legal team must review it before real use (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

What we collect

Name, age, gender, address, mobile number, health details you choose to give (allergies, conditions), category or scheme, and optional ABHA or Ayushman card numbers. For ID proof we keep only the type and the last 4 digits. We never store your full Aadhaar number.

Why

To register you, book your visit, treat you safely and run the hospital. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Reception: identity, address, scheme details, contact.
  • Your doctor: age, sex, allergies, conditions and your visit. Only for patients who have a visit with that doctor.
  • Nurses: clinical details needed for care.
  • Lab and pharmacy: only what is needed for the test or medicine.

Every time staff open a patient record it is written to a tamper-evident audit log.

Your choices

  • Correct your details any time under My family → Edit.
  • Remove a family member you added.
  • Ask the hospital office to withdraw consent or correct a record. Medical records already created must be kept as the law requires.
