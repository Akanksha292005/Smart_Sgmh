You do not need to ask for a bed online. See a doctor in OPD. If the doctor finds that you must stay, the doctor admits you, you get an admission code, and reception gives the bed. Your ward, bed and care team then show on your Visit page.
Emergency (OPD closed, or serious)? Go straight to the Emergency gate (open 24x7) or call 112 / 108. The emergency team admits at once, even without a booking.

How admission works

STEP 1

OPD visit

Book OPD, meet the doctor. The doctor sees the diagnosis, tests and allergy.

STEP 2

Doctor admits

If you need to stay, the doctor chooses the ward and sends the admission. All OPD details go to the ward with you.

STEP 3

Bed at admission desk

Show the admission code and a photo ID with one family attendant. Reception gives the bed.

STEP 4

Ward care

Ward, bed, duty doctor and duty nurse are shown to you and your doctor. If the condition becomes serious, the doctor and the ward's duty staff are alerted at once.

EMERGENCY

When OPD is closed

The emergency team gives triage, admits the patient and calls the duty doctor. Family identity can be added later.
