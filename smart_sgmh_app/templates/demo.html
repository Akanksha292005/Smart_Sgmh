Live Safety Demo (for judges)

1. Something goes wrong→2. System checks automatically→3. Alert pops up for doctor→4. Doctor acknowledges→5. No reply in time = escalated to supervisor
How to show this to judges (2 minutes)
  1. Press Stable patient: no alert, the score is normal.
  2. Press Getting worse: a warning alert appears for the doctor.
  3. Press Critical patient: a red alert pops up with a sound. Then press Acknowledge.
  4. Lab: the Wrong sample button is blocked before a wrong report can reach a patient.
  5. Pharmacy: expired medicine, a medicine that is not prescribed, an allergy medicine and a second click on the correct medicine (duplicate) are all blocked.
  6. Press Critical patient again, do not acknowledge, then press Nobody responded in time: the alert goes to the supervisor.
  7. Finally open the pages below. Use Reset demo to start again.
